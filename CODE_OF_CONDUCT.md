# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and
welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not post credentials, personal data, or
  financial records.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and
intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
Please do not include security vulnerabilities in conduct reports; use the
private process described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Maintainers may remove
content, restrict participation, or close contributions when necessary to
protect the community.
